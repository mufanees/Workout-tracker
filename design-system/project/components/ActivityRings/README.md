# ActivityRings

Today at a glance: Fast, Move and Zone 2 rings with their numbers.

- Solid colours on a neutral `surface-3` track: `blue` for Fast, `ring-move` for Move, `ring-z2` for Zone 2. No glow.
- Any workout earns the day (a check in the centre) even when the Move ring is short of 30 minutes.

Hand-written from `src/styles.css` and the matching component in `src/` (static rendition).

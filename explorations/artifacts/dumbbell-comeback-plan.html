Dumbbell Comeback Plan

A 30-minute strength plan for starting again from scratch, with shoulder-friendly pressing and mobility built into every session. Adapted from the Buff Dudes 12-week dumbbell plan, with the bench work moved to the floor and the warm-up cut to fit the clock. Tap any exercise name (▶) to see form videos for it.

How it works

Schedule

  • Train every other day. That works out to 3–4 sessions a week.
  • Alternate Workout A and Workout B: A, rest, B, rest, A…
  • Rest days: a 10-minute walk plus the warm-up below is plenty.

Supersets keep it to 30 minutes

  • Exercises come in pairs (1a / 1b). Do 1a, then 1b, then rest.
  • Rest 60 s after each pair (75–90 s on the heavy pair in phases 3–4).
  • Finish all sets of pair 1 before moving to pair 2.

Picking weights

  • Choose a weight you could lift for 3–4 more reps than prescribed. Too light is fine in week 1.
  • Rough starting points per dumbbell: floor press 8–10 kg, goblet squat 12–14 kg (one bell), rows 10–12 kg, RDL 10–12 kg, overhead press 5–6 kg.

Progression

  • When you hit the top of the rep range on every set with clean form, add the smallest jump your set allows next session.
  • Upper body moves slower than legs. That's normal.
  • Once something reaches 24 kg, progress with slower lowering (3 s), a pause at the bottom, or more reps.
Shoulder rules

Stiffness that eases as you warm up is fine. Sharp or pinching pain is not: shorten the range or lower the weight, and skip the exercise that day if it doesn't go away. Keep elbows at about 45° from your body when pressing, use a neutral grip (palms facing each other), and only press overhead through a range that feels smooth. If the stiffness gets worse week to week rather than better, get it checked by a physio.

Phase 4 · weeks 10–12

Peak

Pair 1: 4 × 5–8Pairs 2–3: 3 × 8–10Week 12: test

Heaviest phase. A pair of 24 kg dumbbells on the floor press is 48 kg, close to your old 50 kg bench. In week 12, do pair 1 as a test: after the ramp-up, see how many clean reps you get with your heaviest weight, then finish the session as normal.

If a week goes badly

  • Missed a week or more: repeat the last week you completed, at the same weights.
  • Stuck on the same weight for three sessions in a row: do one lighter week (same exercises, about 70% of the weight, 2 sets), then carry on.
  • Low on time: do the warm-up, pair 1 and the cool-down. That's about 20 minutes and still counts.

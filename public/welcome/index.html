The hard cut

How much time do you have?

Pick your minutes. Gloop reshapes the workout to fit, using the same rules as the app. This is Buff Dudes 12 Week, Day 1: five moves, 3 × 10, with a 5-minute warm-up and a 5-minute cool-down.

Warm-up, cool-down Sets Rest

  1. Heavier, fewer repsSets of 10 become sets of 8 with about 16% more weight. Close to the same work.
  2. Pair moves that don't competePush with pull, legs with core, done as supersets with one rest after each round.
  3. Shorter rests90 s between sets, then 60 to 75 s after each round.
  4. Fewer sets, then fewer movesCore and isolation go first. Big lifts go to 2 sets before any is dropped. The cool-down always stays.

How it works

  1. It reads what you give it

    The effort tap after a set, your heart rate from a chest strap, and the clock against your time budget.

  2. It answers on your phone, instantly

    The live coach runs on the device. No internet needed mid-set, so it never waits on a signal.

  3. The AI coach adds the big picture

    Between sessions it reviews your week, answers your questions in chat and plans your program around your time and your kit.

Short days still count

Any session gives the day a showed-up mark, even ten minutes. The app says it plainly: "You showed up today. That counts." A long day closes the ring. A short one keeps your week going.
